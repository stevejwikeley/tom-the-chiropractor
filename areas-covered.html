Getting here

A chiropractor near Shepshed, Quorn, Barrow and Sileby

My clinic is in the centre of Loughborough, so it's a short drive from most of the villages around town. There's a car park opposite, you can book online in under a minute, and there are appointments until 7pm on Mondays and Thursdays and on Saturday mornings.

Finding the clinic

You'll find me on the ground floor of The Work Lounge, 1 Beehive Lane, Loughborough LE11 2FJ. Beehive Lane Car Park is just opposite, around a 30-second walk.

Appointments are booked in advance, so please book a time before you set off. I'm not able to see anyone who turns up on the day.
