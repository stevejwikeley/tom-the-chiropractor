Pricing

Block Booking Terms

Five follow-up treatments paid up front for £200, instead of £45 each. Here is exactly what that does and does not commit you to.

What you get

Five follow-up treatments for £200. Paid in one go, that works out at £40 a treatment rather than the standard £45, so you save £25 across the block.

It covers follow-up treatments only. Your initial consultation is a separate 60-minute appointment at £70, and isn't part of the block.

It expires after 6 months

The block runs for 6 months from the date you pay for it. Any treatments you haven't used by then are no longer available under the block.

Six months is deliberately generous for a course of five treatments, so this shouldn't catch anyone out. If something significant gets in the way, an illness, an injury, a spell away, just tell me and I'll extend it. I'd rather do that than have you lose sessions you've paid for.

Refunds

You can stop at any point and ask for the unused part back. When you do, the treatments you've already had are charged at the standard £45 rate rather than the £40 block rate, and I refund the difference. The discount applies to a completed block, so it doesn't carry over to a partial one.

To ask for a refund, email hello@tomthechiropractor.co.uk or message me. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within a few working days.

It's optional, and it isn't a treatment plan

A block is a way of paying, nothing more. It doesn't commit you to five treatments, and buying one doesn't mean you'll need five. I'll only suggest it once we've assessed you and agreed roughly how many sessions are sensible, and you're welcome to pay per visit instead. If you get better in three, stop at three and ask for the difference back.

Other details

  • Blocks are for one person and can't be transferred to someone else.
  • Normal cancellation notice still applies to each appointment. See the cancellation policy.
  • Cancelling an appointment with proper notice doesn't use up a treatment from your block.

Any questions, just send me a message before you buy.
