Questions people ask me

What is a chiropractor?

A practitioner trained to assess and treat problems with your muscles, joints and spine — usually with hands-on manipulation, mobilisation and exercise advice.

Chiropractor or physio?

Both can help with pain. Chiropractic focuses more on hands-on spinal and joint treatment. If I think physio — or a GP — is the better route, I'll say so and point you there.

Are chiropractors doctors?

No — chiropractors are separately regulated, registered practitioners. I diagnose and treat musculoskeletal problems, and refer you on when something is outside that.
