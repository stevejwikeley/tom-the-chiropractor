Questions people ask me

What is a chiropractor?

A chiropractor is a regulated healthcare professional who assesses and treats problems affecting the muscles, joints, nerves and spine. Treatment can include hands-on techniques, movement advice, education and exercises.

Chiropractor or physio?

Both can help with musculoskeletal pain, and there is overlap between the two. My approach combines hands-on treatment with advice and exercises. If I think a physio, GP or another professional would be more appropriate, I'll tell you and help point you in the right direction.

Are chiropractors regulated?

Yes. Chiropractors in the UK must be registered with the General Chiropractic Council. I'm fully registered and trained at the Welsh Institute of Chiropractic.

Who will I see at my appointments?

You'll see me, Tom, every time. I've learned it's just as important to understand the person as the pain, so I focus on understanding what may be contributing to your pain rather than simply focusing on the symptoms, and I'm with you for every step of your recovery, not just the first visit.

Will it hurt?

Honestly, parts of it can be uncomfortable. Where muscles have tightened up protectively, working through them can bring tenderness in the muscle and the structures around it. That's temporary, and easing those areas is usually what starts to settle things down. Joint work tends to be easier, and some people feel relief straight away. Afterwards it's normal to feel a bit achy for a day or so, much like after exercise.

What's the clicking noise?

That sound is gas releasing from within the joint, the same thing that happens when you crack your knuckles. It isn't bone, and it isn't a sign of damage. If you'd rather avoid that technique altogether, just say so and I'll use something else. There's usually more than one way to treat a problem.

What should I wear?

Something you can move comfortably in. I may need to see or work on the area I'm treating, but you'll never be asked to undress beyond what's necessary, and you're welcome to bring someone with you.

What if I need to cancel?

Just let me know as early as you can, ideally at least 24 hours before. You can use the link in your confirmation email or message me directly. There's more detail in my cancellation policy.
