Clearspace & minimum size

Keep clearspace equal to the circle's stroke width on all sides. Never scale the mark below 24px, or the wordmark below 14px cap-height.

Don't

Don't recolour outside the palette, stretch the circle, add shadows, or set the wordmark in lowercase.
