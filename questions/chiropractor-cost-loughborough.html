My initial consultation is £70 and lasts a full 60 minutes. Follow-up treatments are £45 each, or you can book a block of 5 for £200, which works out at £40 a visit.

Why the first appointment costs more

It's longer, and it covers more than treatment. I'll take a full history, examine how you move, test the relevant nerves and muscles, and explain what I think is going on in plain English before we start any hands-on work. Follow-ups are shorter because that groundwork is already done.

Why prices vary between chiropractors

Chiropractor prices across the UK vary with session length, location and experience. A 60-minute first appointment with a full assessment costs more to deliver than a 20-minute slot, but it also means less guesswork and a clearer plan from day one. Ask what's actually included before comparing prices on paper.

Does insurance cover it?

Some private health insurance policies include chiropractic care, but this varies by provider and policy. If you plan to claim, it's worth checking directly with your insurer before booking, since the details are between you and them.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The online booking system offers Klarna alongside card payment, so you can split the cost into instalments rather than paying it all in one go. Klarna sets its own eligibility and terms, so have a look at what's on offer when you get to checkout.

This is worth knowing if you're weighing up the block of 5: paying £200 upfront usually saves £25 compared with five separate £45 visits, and spreading that £200 with Klarna means you can still get the saving without needing the full amount in your account this month.

Is a block booking worth it?

Only if you actually need several sessions. I won't push you into one, and one of my patients specifically mentioned there being "no pressure to book follow up appointments" — that's deliberate. If your assessment suggests a course of treatment will help, the block simply works out cheaper per visit, and if you're short on cash that month, paying for it with Klarna instalments is worth considering.
