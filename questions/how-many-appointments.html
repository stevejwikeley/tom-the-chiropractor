Questions people ask me

How many chiropractic appointments will I need?

The honest answer

There isn't a fixed number I can give you before I've actually assessed you, and I'd be wary of anyone who does. It varies considerably from person to person, depending on how long the problem has been going on, what's driving it, and how you respond to treatment. I'll give you a realistic estimate after your first assessment, and I'll tell you if things aren't progressing the way I expected.

What actually affects the number

  • How long it's been going on. An acute flare-up — something that "went" a few days ago — often settles more quickly than pain that's been building for months or years.
  • What's driving it. A single tight area responds differently than a problem with several contributing factors.
  • What you do between visits. Following through on exercises and movement advice tends to speed things up.
  • How your body responds. This genuinely varies, and it's part of why I review progress rather than fixing a number in advance.

No pressure to keep booking

Treatment continues only as long as it's genuinely helping. I review progress as we go, and if you're better than expected, or things have stalled and a different approach or a different professional makes more sense, that changes the plan. There's no obligation to commit to a fixed course upfront.

If you do end up needing several sessions

Once your assessment suggests a course of treatment will help, a block of 5 follow-ups works out cheaper per visit than paying one at a time — but it's offered once that's actually the plan, not before.
