Cookie Policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They're used to make sites work, remember your preferences, or understand how the site is used.

Cookies used on this site

This site only sets non-essential cookies with your consent, shown as a banner the first time you visit. If you accept, the following may be set:

  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, used to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, general location and device type), so we can improve it.
  • Google Ads, used to see whether an advert led to a booking, so we know which adverts are worth paying for.

We don't sell any of the information these collect.

If you decline, none of these cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device. Google's tags do still load, but in a restricted mode: they set no cookies, they receive nothing that identifies you or your device, and any advert click reference is stripped out before anything is sent. What reaches Google is an anonymous signal that a page was viewed, which it uses to estimate overall visitor numbers. The site works normally either way.

Third-party services

Some parts of the site link out to or embed third-party services, which may set their own cookies under their own policies once you interact with them:

  • The online booking widget, provided by PracticeHub
  • WhatsApp, when you use the "Message me" links
  • Google Maps, when you view the embedded map or click "Get directions"

Changing your choice

You can change your cookie choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website (usually in your browser's privacy or site settings), which will show the consent banner again on your next visit.

More information

For details on your rights over the personal data these cookies may involve, see our Privacy Policy. For general guidance on cookies, see the ICO's guide to cookies.
