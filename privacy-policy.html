Privacy Policy

Who we are

This website is operated by Tom Wikeley, trading as Tom the Chiropractor, GCC registration number 05268, based at The Work Lounge, 1 Beehive Lane, Loughborough, LE11 2FJ. If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, email hello@tomthechiropractor.co.uk.

Information collected through this website

When you use the online booking system, your name, contact details and appointment information are collected and processed by our booking provider, PracticeHub, in order to schedule and manage your appointment. When you message us via WhatsApp, your phone number and message content are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

If you complete an intake form ahead of your first appointment, the health and contact information you provide is used solely to prepare for and deliver your care, and is kept as part of your clinical record in line with our professional obligations as a GCC-registered chiropractor.

Analytics

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve it. This involves cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy for details, including how to change your choice at any time.

How we use your information

  • To book, manage and remind you of appointments
  • To provide safe and appropriate chiropractic care, including keeping accurate clinical records
  • To respond to enquiries sent by email or WhatsApp
  • To understand and improve how this website is used, where you've given analytics consent

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How long we keep your information

Clinical records are retained in line with General Chiropractic Council and NHS records-retention guidance, which generally means keeping adult patient records for a minimum of 8 years after the last treatment. Booking and enquiry information not linked to an ongoing clinical record is kept only as long as needed for that purpose.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct inaccurate information, and in some circumstances to ask us to delete it or restrict how it's used. To make a request, email hello@tomthechiropractor.co.uk.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.
